Privacy Policy

GPhotos Cam · Effective October 11, 2026

GPhotos Cam is an iPhone camera app that uploads the photos you take to your own Google Photos library. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. The short version: everything stays on your iPhone or goes to Google, and nothing is sent to the app's developer or anyone else.

Information the app uses

  • Camera. The app uses the iPhone camera only while it is open, to show the preview and take the photos you choose to take. It does not record audio or video.
  • Photos and screenshots you capture. Each photo, including its embedded metadata (such as the time it was taken and camera settings), is saved in the app's private storage on your iPhone and then uploaded to your Google Photos library. Screenshots you send with the app's Shortcuts action are handled the same way. The app does not access the Apple Photos library.
  • Google account information. When you sign in, Google Sign-In provides your name, email address and profile picture. The app displays your email address in its Settings so you can see which account is signed in. It does not store or send this information anywhere else.
  • Sign-in tokens. Google Sign-In stores the tokens that let the app upload on your behalf in the iPhone's Keychain.
  • App settings. Your choices, such as photo format and resolution, are saved on your iPhone.

Google user data

The app asks for one Google Photos permission, add to your Google Photos library (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/photoslibrary.appendonly). It uses it only to:

  • upload the photos and screenshots you capture with the app, and
  • create a "Screenshots" album and add your screenshots to it.

The app cannot view, download, edit or delete the photos already in your Google Photos library, and it does not try to.

GPhotos Cam's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

The app sends your photos only to Google Photos, directly from your iPhone, at your request. There is no GPhotos Cam server. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking, and your information is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone else.

Photos stored in Google Photos are covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Storage and deletion

  • By default, a photo is deleted from your iPhone once Google Photos confirms it has received it. If you turn this off in Settings, uploaded copies are kept in the app's storage until you delete them, for example in the Files app.
  • Photos that have not been uploaded yet stay on your iPhone until the upload succeeds.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone, including any photos that have not been uploaded.
  • If iCloud Backup is turned on, your iPhone backup may include the app's stored photos. This is controlled by your iPhone's backup settings.
  • Photos uploaded to Google Photos stay there until you delete them in Google Photos.
  • You can sign out in the app's Settings at any time, and you can remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Children

GPhotos Cam is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
