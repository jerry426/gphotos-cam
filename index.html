GPhotos Cam

An iPhone camera that saves straight to Google Photos.

What it does

GPhotos Cam is a camera app for iPhone. Photos you take with it are uploaded directly from your iPhone to your own Google Photos library. They are never saved to Apple Photos or iCloud Photos.

  • Take photos with the iPhone's cameras, with lens, zoom, focus, exposure, flash and timer controls.
  • Choose HEIC or JPEG, and full or 12 MP resolution.
  • Photos wait in the app's private storage until Google Photos confirms it has them. Failed uploads are retried automatically.
  • An optional Shortcuts action sends screenshots to a "Screenshots" album in Google Photos.

How it uses your Google account

You sign in with Google and grant one Google Photos permission: add to your Google Photos library (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/photoslibrary.appendonly). With it, the app can upload photos and create albums for them. It cannot see, change or delete any photos that are already in your library.

There is no GPhotos Cam server. Your photos and sign-in go only between your iPhone and Google. See the privacy policy for details.

Availability

GPhotos Cam is a personal project and is not available on the App Store.
